# NPC compendium: missing item icons and per-creature pictures

These pictures are for items that sit inside the NPC actors (attacks, abilities) and for creatures that still share a group portrait. Same workflow as `icon-prompts.md`: new ChatGPT image chat, paste the **Master style** once, then one **batch** at a time. Save each sheet as `npc-items-<nn>.png` / `npc-beasts-<nn>.png` and send them over. I slice them into `assets/compendium/npcs/items/<slug>.webp` (item icons) and `assets/compendium/npcs/<entry>.webp` and `<entry>-token.webp` (creature portraits and tokens).

All names refer to the Stargate SG-1 universe (TV series and RPG).
